Where do I find the link?

Google Calendar: on a computer, open Settings, then pick the calendar under "Settings for my calendars" and go to "Integrate calendar". Copy Secret address in iCal format.

Outlook / Microsoft 365: open Settings → Calendar → Shared calendars, then go to "Publish a calendar". Choose the calendar and "Can view all details", press Publish, and copy the ICS link.

Apple iCloud: in the Calendar app, share the calendar as a "Public Calendar" and copy the webcal:// link.

These links are read-only: events show up here but can't be edited from the dashboard. They refresh every {{ config.CALENDAR_STALE_MINUTES }} minutes when someone opens the calendar, or whenever you press Refresh. Anyone with a secret link can read that calendar, so only paste it here.
